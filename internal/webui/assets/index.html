Served on your own machine, on the loopback interface, and not reachable from the network. Nothing on this page is fetched from anywhere else. Close the terminal to stop it.

What this page cannot do. Four things live only on the command line, said here rather than left to be concluded absent. noisecrypt simulate measures which re-encoding qualities a channel survives, which is how the table above was filled in. -kdf-time, -kdf-memory and -kdf-lanes tune the work factor applied to a passphrase, and -crf and -preset tune the video encoder. -passphrase-file and -passphrase-env feed a passphrase from somewhere a script can reach. Start with noisecrypt -help, then noisecrypt <command> -h.
